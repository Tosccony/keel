Keel, portable version
======================

This file makes this copy of Keel portable. Keel keeps everything in this
folder, so you can carry it on an external drive:

  data\     your databases, settings and Keel's logs
  htdocs\   your websites (XAMPP's htdocs)

Your databases and websites are never saved on the computer you plug the
drive into, and Keel keeps working when the drive gets a different letter
on another computer.

Format the drive as NTFS or exFAT. FAT32 can't hold files over 4 GB, and
databases grow.

Before you eject the drive, choose "Stop everything" in Keel (or in its
tray menu). If the drive is pulled out while Keel is running, Keel checks
and recovers your databases the next time it starts.

Don't use the portable version on a computer where other people are signed
in at the same time. exFAT and most external drives have no file
permissions, so they could read your databases and passwords on the drive,
or swap Keel's programs for their own.

If you turn on Perl scripts, Keel adds a small entry for .pl and .cgi files
to your Windows account, and removes it when the web server stops.

Don't delete this file: without it, Keel saves its data on the computer
instead.
